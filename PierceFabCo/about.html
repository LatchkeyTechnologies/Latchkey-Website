About Pierce Fab Co.

Built Different.

After years of building chassis for other companies and even longer as a master welder, in 2026 Kent McGlaun started his own chassis company. Pierce Fab Co. builds custom chassis systems for classic Ford trucks. Our focus is simple: take the trucks people already love and give them the stance, structure, drivability, and performance they always deserved.


We are starting with Ford truck platforms because that is where our passion is. Bumpsides, dentsides, OBS trucks, and the machines that shaped generations of working-class horsepower. Old soul. Modern bones.

Engineering First

Good fabrication starts before the first tube is cut. Geometry, fitment, serviceability, strength, and stance all matter. We build with intent, not guesswork.

Ford Truck Focus

Pierce Fab Co. is focused on classic Ford pickups first: chassis packages, suspension systems, custom fabrication, and rolling foundations built for real street performance.
