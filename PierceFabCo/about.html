Built Different.

Pierce Fab Co. was founded around a simple idea: build chassis and suspension systems the way they should have been built from the beginning.

We combine classic Ford truck styling with modern engineering, performance geometry, fabrication precision, and an obsession with craftsmanship.

Our Philosophy

We believe great fabrication starts with great engineering. Every chassis, crossmember, bracket, and suspension component is designed with purpose.

We are not interested in mass production shortcuts, gimmicks, or throwaway craftsmanship.

We build vehicles that are meant to be driven hard, admired closely, and owned proudly.

Ford Truck Focus

Pierce Fab Co. specializes in classic Ford truck platforms, blending timeless body lines with modern drivability and performance.

From F100s to OBS F150s, our goal is to preserve the soul of these trucks while transforming the way they perform.

Modern suspension. Modern geometry. Real engineering. Classic attitude.
